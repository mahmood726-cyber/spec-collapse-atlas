Spec-Collapse Atlas

Correct inference for multiverse / many-analyst meta-analysis

A multiverse runs many specifications on one dataset. Summarising them by inverse-variance pooling — the usual move — treats correlated re-analyses as independent studies, collapsing the confidence interval and manufacturing robustness the data do not support. The weighted-likelihood interval accounts for both within- and between-specification uncertainty and restores calibrated coverage.

Method: weighted-likelihood is a Gaussian mixture of the per-specification likelihoods (uniform weights); its variance equals mean within-spec variance plus between-spec variance, so it is never narrower than a single specification.
